Marxon Solar Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Marxon Solar mobile application (package: com.marxon.marxonSolar) published by Guangdong Marxon Electronics Co., LTD.

Effective: November 20, 2026English & 中文

1. App purpose

Marxon Solar lets authorized users create or access an account, monitor compatible solar and energy-storage equipment, view stations, collectors, devices, energy statistics and alarms, bind equipment by scanning a code, configure supported devices, and manage subordinate users where the account has permission.

2. Information we collect and process

  • Account and contact data: account name, email address or mobile phone number, country calling code, verification code, and authentication credentials needed for registration, login, password reset, and account support.
  • Precise location and station information: latitude, longitude, country, province, city, area and an optional address when you choose to locate or create a station. Location permission is requested only when a location-related feature is used.
  • Camera and scan data: camera access and the QR/barcode content you choose to scan, used to identify, look up and bind a compatible collector or device.
  • Equipment and energy data: device identifiers and serial numbers, station and device names, installed capacity, operating status, power and energy measurements, alarms, configuration values, and commands you request.
  • User-management data: names, accounts, email addresses, mobile numbers, locations and user roles entered or viewed by authorized manufacturer or administrator accounts.
  • Technical and security data: app version, device and operating-system information, IP-derived region information, access and refresh tokens, request metadata, and limited server logs needed for security, diagnostics and service operation.

Authentication tokens are stored in protected device storage. The app does not include third-party advertising or advertising SDKs and does not sell personal data.

3. How we use information

  • Provide registration, login, account recovery and authorized user management.
  • Connect accounts with stations, collectors and devices and display requested operational data.
  • Locate stations, bind equipment, process alarms, and perform user-requested settings or control operations.
  • Maintain service reliability, prevent misuse, troubleshoot faults, secure accounts, and comply with applicable law.
  • Respond to support, privacy, safety and legal requests.

4. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be processed by our backend infrastructure and service providers only as needed to operate, secure and support Marxon Solar. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, rights, property or service security. Depending on where you use the app, information may be processed in countries or regions other than your own, subject to applicable safeguards.

5. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the service. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted network transport and protected local token storage. No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can deny or revoke camera and location permissions in system settings. Core monitoring may remain available, but scanning or automatic location features may not work.
  • You may correct profile or station information where the app provides that function.
  • You may sign out and clear locally stored session data.
  • To request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other rights available under applicable law, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify account ownership before acting.

7. Children

Marxon Solar is intended for equipment owners, installers, distributors and other authorized users, not for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal data in violation of applicable law.
